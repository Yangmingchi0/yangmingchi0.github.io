Research interests

I study how large language models and the agents built on them behave when they face conflicting or malicious instructions. I’m interested in using mechanistic interpretability to understand how models make safety-relevant decisions, and in improving the safety of agents that use external tools.

  • Mechanistic interpretability
  • AI safety
  • LLM and agent security
  • Safety post-training
